Investigations

All syncope: 12-lead ECG (immediately), BM, U&E, FBC, troponin (hs-troponin at 0h and 3h), lying/standing BP

Targeted: Echo (structural cardiac), 24h/7d Holter (arrhythmia), tilt table testing (vasovagal), CT head (if post-ictal/focal neuro/head injury), EEG (if seizure suspected), CXR

ECG — look for: QTc prolongation (>450ms M, >460ms F), Brugada pattern, WPW (delta waves), complete heart block, ventricular arrhythmia, LBBB (new), epsilon wave (ARVC), T-wave inversion V1-V3

⚠️ Exertional syncope, new ECG changes, or CSRS ≥3 → admit for cardiac monitoring
1

Immediate

  • 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes, continuous monitoring if cardiac suspected
  • IV access, BM, bloods including hs-troponin
  • Lying/standing BP (postural drop >20/10mmHg = orthostatic)
2

Low Risk (CSRS <0) — Vasovagal

  • Discharge with safety-netting: avoid triggers, maintain hydration, avoid prolonged standing
  • DVLA guidance: must not drive for 6 months if unexplained syncope (check current DVLA rules)
  • Refer GP for further follow-up; outpatient Holter if recurrent
3

High Risk (CSRS ≥3) — Cardiac

  • Admit for telemetry/continuous cardiac monitoring
  • Cardiology referral. Echocardiogram. Extended Holter or implantable loop recorder.
  • Treat underlying cause: antiarrhythmics, PPM, ICD, valve surgery as appropriate
4

Orthostatic Hypotension

  • Review and reduce/stop offending medications (antihypertensives, diuretics, alpha-blockers)
  • Increase fluid/salt intake. Compression stockings. Rise slowly.
  • If autonomic failure: fludrocortisone / midodrine (specialist)

Immediate

  • BM — if <4mmol/L: Glucogel PO or 50ml 50% glucose IV or Glucagon 1mg IM

2

Investigations

  • Urine dip ± MC&S, CXR, ECG, CT head (if focal neuro / head trauma / anticoagulated / first presentation)
  • ABG if SpO₂ low, respiratory compromise, or severe metabolic derangement
3

Delirium Management (NICE NG20)

  • Treat underlying cause (infection, pain, metabolic)
  • Non-pharmacological: reorientation, familiar faces, hearing aids/glasses, avoid restraint, maintain day/night cycle, early mobilisation
  • Pharmacological (only if risk of harm): Haloperidol 0.5-1mg PO/IM (avoid in Parkinson's / Lewy body — use Quetiapine 12.5-25mg PO)
  • Avoid benzodiazepines (worsen delirium) EXCEPT alcohol withdrawal

1

SAH Protocol

  • CT head STAT (sensitivity ~98% within 6h, lower after)
  • If CT negative + onset <6h: consider CTA or LP at 12h
  • LP at ≥12h: xanthochromia (spectrophotometry — not just visual inspection)
  • If SAH confirmed: neurosurgery referral immediately. Nimodipine 60mg PO q4h (vasospasm prevention).
2

Migraine (BASH Guidelines)

  • Acute: Aspirin 900mg PO + Metoclopramide 10mg PO/IV (or Prochlorperazine 10mg PO/3mg buccal)
  • If fails: Sumatriptan 50-100mg PO (or 6mg SC) — avoid if hemiplegic migraine / cardiovascular disease
  • Severe/refractory: Sumatriptan 6mg SC + Prochlorperazine 12.5mg IM
  • IV option: IV paracetamol 1g + IV metoclopramide 10mg + fluids
3

Cluster Headache

  • 100% O₂ via non-rebreathe mask 12-15 L/min for 15-20 min (very effective)
  • Sumatriptan 6mg SC
4

Giant Cell Arteritis (GCA)

  • ESR + CRP urgently. Temporal artery biopsy arranged within 2 weeks.
  • If visual symptoms: Methylprednisolone 500mg-1g IV (same day)
  • Without visual symptoms: Prednisolone 40-60mg PO OD immediately — do not wait for biopsy

CT Positive — Intracranial Haemorrhage
